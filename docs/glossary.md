# Glossary

Terms used across the showcase kit, in alphabetical order.

### Baseline
What you compare your result against: the existing tool, the status quo, a simple method, or a control group. A number without a baseline can't show improvement.

### Claude skill
A packaged set of instructions that teaches Claude one task. This repository's skill helps a team prepare its showcase deck. It's optional; the [fill-in templates](../template/README.md) cover the same format without AI.

### Falsifiable
A question or hypothesis is falsifiable if some result could prove it wrong. "Our model beats the baseline by 5 F1 points" is falsifiable; "our approach is promising" is not.

### IDEA
Northeastern's student-led venture accelerator. Innovation teams that want to continue as a startup may apply to it after the showcase.

### Innovation Team
See [Track](#track).

### Intake proposal
The plan a team submitted at the start of the semester, which Launch Lab reviewed when accepting the project. It usually covers Project, Resources, Milestones and Team. The showcase reports against it. See the [team guide](../template/README.md#2-your-intake-proposal-and-milestones).

### Judge pass
The Claude skill's step of asking a team the hardest questions a faculty judge would ask, before building the deck. Teams working by hand do the same with [judge-questions.md](../references/judge-questions.md).

### Launch Lab
The Khoury College graduate-student project club that runs these teams and the showcase. Its branding shows "NU Launch Labs".

### Leakage
When information from the test data leaks into training or tuning, making results look better than they are. Research teams should say how they prevented it.

### Milestone
A checkpoint set in the intake proposal, with a **pass criterion**: a concrete test of whether it was met. Statuses at the showcase:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PASSED** | The criterion was met |
| **MISSED** | Tried and not met |
| **STOPPED** | The team deliberately ended this work, for example after early results. A decision, not a failure |
| **PARTIAL** | Partly met |
| **PENDING** | Depends on something outside the team's control (an approval, a data release) that hasn't happened yet |

### Pass criterion
See [Milestone](#milestone).

### Research Team
See [Track](#track).

### Showcase
The end-of-semester event where every Launch Lab team presents to faculty judges, sponsors, recruiters and students. It's a final review of the semester's work, not an idea pitch.

### Spine
The fixed sequence of slides for a track. The Innovation and Research templates each follow one.

### Stubbed
Part of a system that's planned or faked for now (hard-coded, mocked) rather than really built. The architecture slide marks these with dashed boxes.

### Threats to validity
Reasons a research conclusion might be wrong even if the numbers look good: a small sample, one dataset, leakage, a biased setup.

### Track
The kind of project a team runs. **Innovation Teams** (Track One) are startup-style projects that go from idea to working demo in a semester. **Research Teams** (Track Two) go from a question to findings. Each track has its own template.
